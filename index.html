what is sunhacks?

Founded in 2018, sunhacks is an annual hackathon (technology ideathon, coding marathon, software/hardware prototyping marathon, etc) based out of Arizona State University. Designed to support students of all skills levels in their personal development, sunhacks also facilitates workshops, mentorship by working professionals, connection with the broader tech community, and a peer-based community of practice oriented towards helping students mutually succeed and achieve their goals.

faq

We know there can be a lot of unknowns before an event launches. If you still have questions, email us at team@sunhacks.io.

when is the next sunhacks?

sunhacks will return in November 2026. Exact dates and event details will be announced here when they are finalized.

who can hack?

sunhacks is built for students of all skill levels. Full eligibility, registration, and attendance details will be shared when registration opens.

do I need a team?

Participating solo will be allowed, but we encourage you to come with teammates or find people to build with at the event. Full team size and project submission details will be announced closer to launch.

I don't know how to code / I don't think I'm good enough

You are absolutely welcome. sunhacks is an open community, and we build the event around helping new hackers ask questions, learn quickly, and find support.

can I sponsor, mentor, or partner?

Yes. Reach out to team@sunhacks.io and the organizing team will point you to the right next step.

can I request additional accommodations?

Absolutely. Accommodation request details will be included with registration, and you can always email team@sunhacks.io with questions.
