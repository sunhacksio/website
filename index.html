A hackathon is a place to try something new. Bring an idea, a half-finished thought, or just yourself. We’ll bring a community ready to build with you.

what is sunhacks?

Founded in 2018, sunhacks is an annual student hackathon based at Arizona State University. It’s a space for problem solvers, creators, and developers of every skill level to turn their curiosity into something real.

Through workshops, mentorship from working professionals, and a community that wants you to succeed, you’ll learn together, make connections, and build software, hardware, or something entirely your own.

04 a little trail guidance

curious?
you’ll fit right in.

Every adventure starts with a few questions. Here are some answers to get you going.

ask us anything ↗
when is the next sunhacks?

sunhacks will return in November 2026. Exact dates, venue, and event details will be announced here when they’re finalized.

who can hack?

sunhacks is built for students of all skill levels. Full eligibility, registration, and attendance details will be shared when registration opens.

do I need a team?

You can participate solo, but we encourage you to come with teammates or find people to build with at the event. Team size and project submission details will be announced closer to launch.

what if I don’t know how to code?

You are absolutely welcome. sunhacks is an open community, and we build the event around helping new hackers ask questions, learn quickly, and find support. You don’t need to have it all figured out to get started.

can I sponsor, mentor, or partner?

Yes! Reach out to team@sunhacks.io and the organizing team will point you to the right next step.

can I request accommodations?

Absolutely. Accommodation request details will be included with registration, and you can always email team@sunhacks.io with questions.
